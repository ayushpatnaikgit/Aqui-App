Aqui turns your Android phone into a particulate-matter monitor. Plug a Nova Fitness SDS011 (or SDS021) air quality sensor into the phone's USB port and Aqui shows live PM2.5 and PM10 readings, in µg/m³, a few seconds later.

WHAT YOU SEE
• PM2.5 in large type, with its air quality category (Good, Moderate, Sensitive groups and so on) and a one-line hint
• PM10 alongside, with its own category
• A rolling average of the last three readings and the time of the latest one
• A log of what the app is doing: waiting for the sensor, asking for USB permission, connected, disconnected. A developer switch shows the raw sensor frames.

WHAT YOU NEED
• An SDS011 or SDS021 sensor with its USB serial adapter (CH340)
• A USB-C or micro-USB OTG adapter, depending on your phone
• An Android phone that supports USB host (OTG)
The app only displays readings while a sensor is connected. The sensor, a short cable and a 3D-printable case cost a fraction of a commercial monitor. Parts list, case files and build videos are at aqui.xkdr.org.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No internet permission: the app cannot send anything anywhere
• No location, no account, no ads, no analytics
• Readings stay in memory while the app is open and are not stored
The only permission Aqui asks for is access to the USB sensor you plug in.

OPEN SOURCE
Aqui is open source. The code is at github.com/ayushpatnaikgit/Aqui-App.
